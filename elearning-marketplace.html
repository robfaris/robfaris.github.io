CMS Health Insurance Marketplace eLearning Training

Within the Health & Human Services (HHS) Center for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), the center for Consumer Information and Insurance Oversight (CCIIO) is tasked with training and informing health insurance agents and brokers on how best to serve Marketplace consumers. Each year there is a mandatory training all Marketplace agents and brokers must take to be registered within the Marketplace.

The Problem

The previous yearly training from another contractor had received complaints about interactivity, and the client had little feedback on whether the users were learning from the training, or simply skipping through each year. They asked for a full redesign with more engaging elements, using Adobe Captivate, the software used by the previous contractor.

The Solution

Working with health insurance SMEs creating information and quizzes for the training, I created several sets of designs with different styles, focusing on user accessibility and clarity, operating within CMS branding and style guide. Once a design was chosen, I built the responsive design by hand within Adobe Captivate, and integrated SCORM 1.3 to track quiz responses. The client loved the final design, and after we moved the trainings to Adobe Articulate, I still use many of the included design elements.
